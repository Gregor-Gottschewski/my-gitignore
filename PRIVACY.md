## Network connections
To provide its main function, the app connects to GitHub:
- `https://github.com/github/gitignore` – to download the templates.
- `https://api.github.com` – to check once a day whether the templates changed.

When the app contacts GitHub, GitHub receives technical data that is part of every internet request, such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic request information. I do not control this data and have no access to it. GitHub's handling of it is described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app sends no personal data and no content of your gitignore files.
The app makes no other network connections.
## Clipboard
When you tap "Copy", the generated .gitignore text is placed on your clipboard. The app does not read the clipboard.

## Legal basis, recipients and storage
- **Personal data I process:** none. The connections to GitHub are made directly from your Mac to GitHub.
- **Legal basis for the connection to GitHub:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the download and update of templates is the core function of the app you purchased) and, where applicable.
- **Recipient:** GitHub, Inc., which receives your IP address and request data as described above and acts as a separate controller under its own privacy statement.
- **Transfer to third countries:** GitHub, Inc. is located in the USA. According to GitHub, transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Storage period:** I store no personal data. The app's data on your Mac (templates, your gitignore files, settings) stays there until you delete it or remove the app.
- **Provision of data:** Providing data is neither legally nor contractually required. Without a connection to GitHub the app cannot download or update templates.
- **Automated decisions:** there is no automated decision-making or profiling.
